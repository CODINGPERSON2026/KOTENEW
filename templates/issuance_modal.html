HID DigitalPersona 4500 Hardware Setup & Troubleshooting
HID 4500 Optical Reader Hardware Specs:
USB Vendor ID: 0x05BA | Product ID: 0x000A / 0x000B | Optical Glass Sensor

🔧 Device Detection & Connection Steps:

  1. USB Cable Check: HID 4500 fingerprint reader ko PC ke USB port par plug karein. Device ke top optical glass par Blue LED light honi chahiye.
  2. WebUSB Browser Pairing (Recommended): Chrome ya Edge browser me Pair USB button par click karein aur popup list me DigitalPersona 4500 Fingerprint Reader choose karke Connect karein.
  3. Driver Check (Windows Device Manager): Device Manager me verify karein ki DigitalPersona U.are.U 4500 Fingerprint Reader driver properly installed hai.
  4. Windows Hello (WBF Service): Windows Settings -> Accounts -> Sign-in options me Fingerprint (Windows Hello) enabled hone par system automatically HID 4500 fingerprint sensor ko verify karta hai.
Testing Mode / Operator Fallback:

Agar kisi demonstration ya testing machine par physical HID 4500 device connected nahi hai, toh aap direct Scan Fingerprint click karke Operator Test Mode trigger kar sakte hain taaki KOTE weapon OUT process na ruke!
